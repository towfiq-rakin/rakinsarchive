## Question 3
###### a. Name the International Environment Organizations (IEOs). Explain the functions of IEOs on protecting environment. <span style="float: right; ">07</span>
International Environmental Organizations (IEOs) are institutions, either intergovernmental or non-governmental, that work across national borders to address environmental issues. These organizations coordinate global efforts to protect the environment, combat climate change, conserve biodiversity, and promote sustainable development.

**Examples of Prominent IEOs:**
1. UNEP – United Nations Environment Programme 
2. IPCC – Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change
3. IUCN – International Union for Conservation of Nature
4. GEF – Global Environment Facility

**Functions of IEOs in Environmental Governance** 
1. **Facilitating Cooperation:** IEOs promote dialogue and collaboration among nations.
2. **Policy and Norm Setting:** IEOs facilitate international treaties and set environmental standards.
3. **Knowledge Production:** IEOs conduct research and disseminate environmental knowledge.
4. **Capacity Building:** IEOs provide training and technology transfer to countries.
5. **Financial Assistance:** IEOs distribute funds for environmental projects.

###### b. Explain the effect of Rio Earth Summit (1992) and Paris Agreement (2015) on global environment. <span style="float: right; ">08</span>
**Rio Earth Summit**
1. **Agenda 21:** A comprehensive action plan for sustainable development.
2. **Rio Declaration:** 27 principles, including the precautionary principle, polluter pays, and common but differentiated responsibilities (CBDR).
3. **Three major legally binding agreements initiated:**
   - UN Framework Convention on Climate Change (UNFCCC)
   - Convention on Biological Diversity (CBD)
   - UN Convention to Combat Desertification (UNCCD)
1. Also led to the **strengthening of UNEP** and creation of the Commission on Sustainable Development (CSD).  

**Paris Agreement (2015)**
1. **Nationally Determined Contributions (NDCs):** Each country sets its own emission targets.
2. **Transparency and Review Mechanisms:** Common framework for reporting progress.
3. **No top-down enforcement**, but relies on transparency, peer pressure, and periodic reviews to encourage compliance.
4. Includes provisions for **climate finance, adaptation, loss and damage**, and **capacity building**.
5. Demonstrated a shift from legally binding emission targets (like in the Kyoto Protocol) to a more **flexible, inclusive model**.
6. Marked a **global political consensus** on the urgency of climate change.
###### c. Discuss the applications of AI in Environmental Monitoring. <span style="float: right; ">05</span>
Applications of AI in Environmental Monitoring:  
1. **Climate Change**
- AI models predict extreme weather (e.g., Google’s Flood Hub).
- Carbon footprint tracking (e.g., BCG’s CO2 AI).
1. **Biodiversity Conservation**
- Camera traps + AI identify endangered species (e.g., Wildlife Insights).
- Acoustic monitoring for anti-poaching (e.g., Rainforest Connection).
1. **Pollution Control**
- AI maps air quality in real time (e.g., IBM’s Green Horizon).
- Plastics detection in oceans (The Ocean Cleanup + ML).
