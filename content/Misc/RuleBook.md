## 🏆 Contest Rules

### 1. Bring Your Own Laptop & Charger *(Preferable)*

Contestants are **strongly encouraged to bring their own laptop and charger**. Limited machines may be available on-site, but bringing your own device ensures a smooth experience.
### 2. No LLM or Web Surfing

The use of **ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, Google**, or any external websites during the contest is **strictly prohibited**.

### 3. No Communication

Do **not talk to**, look at the screens of, or manipulate other contestants in any way.
### 4. Username Format

Your platform username must strictly follow the format:

```
Full_Name_Batch_Year
```

> **Example:** `Mihir_Bormon_BCSE_23`
> ⚠️ Accounts failing to comply will be **disqualified**.
### 5. Zero Tolerance for Plagiarism

Code sharing in any form will result in **immediate disqualification**. System-level plagiarism checkers will be deployed post-contest.

### 6. Device Restrictions

**Mobile phones, smartwatches, and unauthorized electronic devices** must be turned off and kept inside your bag during the contest.
### 7. No Pre-written Code

Using **pre-written code templates or hard copies** is not allowed. All code must be written from scratch during the contest.

### 8. Punctuality

Contestants must be in their seats **at least 15 minutes before** the contest begins.
### 9. Platform Clarifications

If you need help understanding a problem description, use the **platform's Announcement feature** instead of asking invigilators for logic explanations.
### 10. Time Penalties

Standard **ICPC Time Penalty** rules apply.

### 11. Technical Issues

If you face hardware, network, or platform issues, **silently raise your hand** for an invigilator to assist you.
