## Online Privacy Protection vs Public Safety

Privacy is the right to be left alone and free from surveillance and unreasonable personal intrusions. Online privacy protection involves controlling how someone's personal information is collected, stored and shared among third parties over internet. However, the right to privacy is not absolute and must be balanced according to the need of society.

Public safety is mostly ensured by the government, which involves protecting citizens from crime, terrorism and harm. It ensures national security, creating a safe environment for the people to survive.

Now, answering the question, "Are online privacy protections more important than public safety?" well, the choice is not binary; we simply cannot choose privacy protection over public safety or vice versa. Balancing both is crucial for individual safety and privacy.

According to utilitarianism theory, reducing privacy protection can be justified if it prevents harm to many people.
As a cybersecurity enthusiast, I think reducing privacy protection can lead to harmful consequences such as data breaches and public surveillance, which ultimately compromise public safety. On the other hand, government and national security agencies should have some control over online privacy to surveil against terrorism and illegal activities but only when necessary and legally justified. In short, online privacy protection should be maintained strictly but can be compromised by appropriate authority to ensure public safety, justified by legal boundaries.

## Super power during campus hours

If granted one super power to use during university hours, the most logical choice in  my mind is the ability to pause time or localized time dilation. This power separates the thinking process from the strict schedules of lectures and exams.
Consider stopping the clock while a professor explains a complex data structure or algorithm, providing the opportunity to understand the logic step-by-step. Similarly, freezing time during an exam allows for careful code debugging and verification without any pressure. Ultimately, this ability changes time from a strict limit into a useful tool, ensuring complete comprehension always happens before the class ends.
